# Creative direction (from the owner)

## Direction: Quiet luxury
For premium services, hospitality, private membership, and refined brands.
Inspiration: Aesop. Restrained colors, elegant photography, subtle typography, carefully controlled negative space.

## Art director brief
The website must feel deliberately designed by an experienced human design team, not generated from a
conventional AI website template. Every visual decision must serve the content, brand, and intended audience.

Avoid generic AI aesthetics:
- No default purple/blue gradients, pastel glows, glassmorphism, or floating decorative blobs.
- No automatic use of large rounded rectangles, pill badges, or repetitive card grids.
- No predictable centered hero followed by three feature cards, testimonials, and a generic CTA.
- No arbitrary gradient text, emojis as icons, excessive shadows, or decorative illustrations without purpose.
- No generic startup language such as "Unlock the power of," "Seamlessly transform," or "Reimagine the future."
- No uniformly spacious sections with identical visual hierarchy.
- No invented statistics, logos, testimonials, or credibility claims.
- Do not interpret minimalism as enormous whitespace and a handful of oversized words.

Art direction:
- Establish a distinct visual identity appropriate to the actual organization and its audience.
- Draw from editorial design, institutional publications, sophisticated print typography.
- Deliberate typographic hierarchy, restrained color, considered density, strong alignment, coherent grid.
- Allow asymmetry, contrast, compact information-rich layouts, and unexpected composition when justified.
- Choose typography for its personality and readability rather than automatically selecting Inter.
- Use border radii, shadows, and icons sparingly and with clear purpose.
- Vary section structures according to content. Do not repeat one component pattern throughout the page.
- Use authentic, contextually relevant imagery only when it materially strengthens the page.

Process: identify audience and personality; define art direction, type scale, palette, layout system, imagery
policy; design each section according to its content; implement responsively and accessibly; inspect rendered
site at desktop and mobile; critique anything generic, formulaic, or AI-looking, and revise.

Quality standard: a bespoke website created by a skilled design studio. Distinctiveness from thoughtful
decisions and specificity, not arbitrary visual complexity.

## Creative director review (run after build)
Review the rendered website as a skeptical senior creative director. Identify the 10 strongest signs that the
design was generated by AI. Examine typography, color, spacing, generic components, visual repetition,
information density, and whether the page has an identifiable design personality. Revise the implementation to
resolve these problems. Make substantial improvements to visual composition, not merely cosmetic changes.
Preserve functionality and accessibility. Do not add visual complexity solely to make the design different.
